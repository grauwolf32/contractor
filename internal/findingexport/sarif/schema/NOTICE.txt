Abstract: This document defines a standard format for the output of static analysis tools. The format is referred to as the “Static Analysis Results Interchange Format” and is abbreviated as SARIF. Status: This document was last revised or approved by the OASIS Static Analysis Results Interchange Format (SARIF) TC on the above date. The level of approval is also listed above.
