## Bounded history reads (A4/A5, R10/R13)

The existing coordinator already continues through two checkpoints and preserves
the logical invocation. Acceptance found two violations of its page-work contract:

- Reading a page copied all 4,096 group references through `archive.groups` and
  then copied the entire compacted prefix again.
- Fragment retrieval sliced the entire remaining projection before selecting a
  32-KiB fragment, and encoded the full projection to test whether it fit a page.

Two new tests fail on the original implementation: a first page visits 4,096
groups, and the large-result case slices 4,194,963 characters for one small page.
The correction keeps the existing archive owner, reads the ordinal index directly
with the committed prefix bound, and searches only a page-sized substring at the
requested offset. Uncompacted groups remain unavailable. No limits, cursors,
publication authority, wire types or failure classifications change.

Regression coverage:

- `test_history_reads_do_not_copy_the_full_4096_group_archive`: pages from the
  start, middle and end of a real admitted 4,096-group archive visit at most
  eight groups plus two validation/lookahead accesses. The uncommitted final
  group is excluded.
- `test_large_history_fragments_copy_and_encode_only_a_page`: three returned
  continuation positions in a real 4-MiB tool-result group never slice or encode
  more than 32,768 source characters per operation; each returned JSON page
  remains within 32 KiB.

The existing multibyte reconstruction, byte/group ceilings, redaction, Skill
disclosure, invocation scoping, marker and cancellation tests remain applicable.
All 54 focused coordinator/ADK tests pass after the correction.
